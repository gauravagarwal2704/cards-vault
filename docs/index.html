01

Encrypted where it matters.

Sensitive card fields use AES-256-GCM encryption, with keys held in platform secure storage. Photo attachments are encrypted, too.

02

A familiar way to unlock.

Device biometrics or your device credential protect access. Card details stay masked until revealed, then hide automatically after 30 seconds.

03

Offline comes naturally.

Android card recognition runs on your phone. The production app has no Internet permission. Sharing and support open the external app you choose.

04

Backups with a key you keep.

Password-encrypted exports use AES-GCM and Argon2id. Choose a password of at least 12 characters and keep it safe; it cannot be recovered.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Nice to know.

Small details. Clear answers.

What do I need to use CardVault?

An Android phone running Android 8.0 or later. Manual entry works without a camera or NFC. To tap a card, you’ll need NFC hardware, NFC enabled, and a compatible contactless EMV card.

Can I use it to make payments?

CardVault is a personal card organizer. It helps you save and find card information; it does not make contactless payments. NFC scanning reads supported card details and cannot read your printed CVV/CVC or PIN.

Does my wallet sync to the cloud?

Your saved wallet stays on your device. You can create a password-encrypted backup and transfer it yourself. There is no CardVault account, automatic cloud sync, or server-side recovery.

What about screenshots and widgets?

Protected Android app windows block screenshots and screen recording. Home-screen widgets are outside app lock: selected previews and artwork can be visible to others and captured by the launcher. Full numbers, expiry dates, security codes, and notes are not copied from saved fields into widget previews.

Custom card backgrounds are image files in private app storage, without the additional encryption used for sensitive card fields. Details embedded in artwork remain visible wherever that artwork is displayed.

Are there ads or locked features?

There are no ads or analytics. If you’d like to support development, optional Supporter Stars are available through Google Play. They do not restrict or unlock functional app features.

Is CardVault open source?

Yes. CardVault is open source, with its source code available on GitHub. Explore how the app works, report issues, or contribute improvements.

How can I get help or share feedback?

Email agarwalgaurav.apps@gmail.com or open a GitHub issue. Send security concerns privately by email. Please leave real card details and credentials out of reports.
